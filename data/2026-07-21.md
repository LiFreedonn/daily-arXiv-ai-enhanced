### [1] [An Empirical Study of Handcrafted Feature Learning and Convolutional Neural Networks for Facial Expression Recognition](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15288)
*Chethiya Galkaduwa*

Main category: cs.CV

TL;DR: 本文比较了HOG+SVM、LBP+逻辑回归和轻量级CNN在FER-2013、CK+、KDEF三个表情识别数据集上的性能。CNN在复杂数据上表现最佳，HOG在受控环境下表现良好，LBP在所有数据集上表现最差。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 虽然CNN在现代面部表情识别中占主导地位，但手工特征描述子（如HOG、LBP）作为经典基线仍有参考价值。本文旨在系统比较这些方法在不同复杂度数据集上的表现，以揭示其适用边界。

Method: 采用三种方法：HOG+SVM、LBP+逻辑回归、轻量级CNN，在FER-2013（复杂）、CK+（受控）、KDEF（受控）三个数据集上进行实验对比。

Result: CNN整体性能最佳，尤其在复杂数据集上优势明显；HOG在受控环境下表现强劲；LBP在所有数据集上表现较差。数据集复杂度显著影响性能。

Conclusion: 鲁棒的特征学习对于真实世界的面部表情识别至关重要，数据集复杂度是影响识别性能的关键因素。

Abstract: Facial expression recognition is an important computer vision task with applications in human--computer interaction, mental health monitoring, driver alert systems, and behavioral analysis. While convolutional neural networks (CNNs) dominate modern facial expression recognition, handcrafted feature descriptors such as Histogram of Oriented Gradients (HOG) and Local Binary Patterns (LBP) remain useful classical baselines. This study compares HOG with Support Vector Machine (SVM), LBP with Logistic Regression, and a lightweight CNN across three facial expression datasets: FER-2013, CK+, and KDEF. The results show that CNNs achieve the best overall performance, particularly on more complex data, while HOG performs strongly in controlled environments. LBP performs poorly across all datasets. The study highlights that dataset complexity significantly affects performance and that robust feature learning is essential for real-world facial expression recognition.

### [2] [AC-VLA: Robust Out-of-Distribution Action Execution via Compositional Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15714)
*Xiaojiang Peng,Kai Peng,Jie Lu,Zheng Lian,Zitong YU,Xiaobo Wang*

Main category: cs.RO

TL;DR: 提出AC-VLA框架，通过动作组合学习和状态条件非对称掩码策略解决VLA模型在OOD任务中的轨迹过拟合和感知捷径问题，无需修改模型架构即可提升组合泛化能力。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: VLA模型在端到端机器人操作中表现优异，但在子任务重新组合的分布外场景中泛化能力差，存在轨迹过拟合和感知捷径两个相互强化的失效模式。

Method: AC-VLA包含两个架构无关的组件：(i) 组合学习模块，利用LLM驱动的指令分解器和本体轨迹对齐器生成密集子任务监督，结合完整演示与分解数据的混合训练；(ii) 状态条件非对称掩码策略，在夹爪闭合阶段抑制腕部视角输入，强制全局语义定位。

Result: 在π_{0.5}上实例化，在LIBERO和LIBERO-OOD基准上，组合OOD任务绝对提升约28%，同时保持近完美的分布内性能。

Conclusion: AC-VLA有效解决了VLA模型的组合泛化问题，且无架构修改，可直接集成到任何VLA主干中，为提升机器人操作模型的泛化能力提供了轻量级方案。

Abstract: Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models excel at end-to-end robotic manipulation but struggle with out-of-distribution (OOD) generalization when familiar sub-tasks are recombined in unseen configurations. We identify two mutually reinforcing failure modes: \emph{trajectory overfitting}, where models overfit to holistic trajectory patterns rather than compositional sub-skill semantics; and \emph{perceptual shortcut}, where action tokens over-rely on wrist-view textures at the expense of global spatial grounding. To address both, we introduce \textbf{AC-VLA}, a plug-and-play Action Compositional learning framework comprising two architecture-agnostic components: \textbf{(i)} a compositional learning module that uses an LLM-driven instruction decomposer and a proprioceptive trajectory aligner to generate dense sub-task supervision, followed by mixed training on complete demonstrations and decomposed data to endow the model with compositional generalization; and \textbf{(ii)} a state-conditioned asymmetric masking strategy that suppresses wrist-view inputs during closed-gripper phases, enforcing global semantic grounding. All components are architectural modification-free and directly integrable into any VLA backbone. Instantiated on $π_{0.5}$ and evaluated on LIBERO and LIBERO-OOD benchmarks, AC-VLA achieves a ~28% absolute improvement on compositional OOD tasks while maintaining near-perfect in-distribution performance.
