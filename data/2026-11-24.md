### [1] [Decomposing images in a PRISM: A Single-Source Multi-View Framework for Image Representation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26726)
*Eduardo Campos,Willam Cardoso,Diego Furtado,Solange Oliveira,Alneu Lopes*

Main category: cs.CV

TL;DR: 提出PRISM框架，通过显式结构图把单幅图像变换成具有不同归纳偏置的多视图表示，并给出衡量各视图贡献的协议；其手工实例PRISM-HD在7个数据集上优于WND-CHARM，且与Fisher Vector、微调深度网络具有互补性。

Motivation: 多视图学习依赖互补视图，但许多数据集只有单一原始信息源，因此需要研究如何从单一来源构造多个有用视图。

Method: PRISM通过显式结构图（structural maps）将图像转换为具有不同归纳偏置的表示，并提供量化各视图贡献的评测协议；PRISM-HD为其手工设计实例，用表示特定的描述子汇总各构造视图，在固定特征预算下与WND-CHARM、Fisher Vector及微调深度网络进行对比与等权/无参数融合。

Result: 固定特征预算分析显示结构视图的增益不能仅由特征维度解释；PRISM-HD单独在全部7个数据集上超过WND-CHARM；与Fisher Vector等权融合在艺术类数据集上比最佳单一表示提升5–8个百分点；与微调深度网络按固定无参数规则融合后，4/7数据集提升，其余3个统计上无变化。

Conclusion: 从单源图像构造的多种结构视图可提供与学习式深度表示互补的预测信号，是一种无需深度网络也能受益的通用表示增强思路。

Abstract: Multi-view learning has shown that combining complementary views can improve predictive performance, yet many datasets provide a single raw source of information. How can multiple useful views be constructed from one source? We propose PRISM (Plural Representations of Images via Structural Maps), a framework that transforms an image into representations with distinct inductive biases through explicit structural maps, together with a protocol for measuring what each view contributes. We evaluate PRISM-HD, a hand-designed instantiation of PRISM in which the constructed views are summarized through representation-specific descriptors, on three artistic and four non-artistic image datasets. Analyses at a fixed feature budget show that the gains of the structural views are not explained by feature dimensionality alone. PRISM-HD by itself outperforms WND-CHARM on all seven datasets. Moreover, PRISM-HD and Fisher Vector provide complementary predictive signals: their equal-weight combination improves over the better individual representation by 5--8 percentage points on the artistic datasets. When combined with fine-tuned deep networks through a fixed parameter-free fusion rule, the PRISM-based representation improves performance on four of seven datasets and leaves it statistically unchanged on the remaining three, indicating that the constructed views provide predictive signals that are complementary to learned deep representations.
