Method: 构建 MeshSuite 基准：定义 Standard Deployment（不透明网格管线＋顶点色＋硬件 Z-buffer）与 Dedicated Deployment（方法专属引擎实现，保留外观与合成特性）；在两个部署数据集上做直接对比；对 mesh splatting 方法审计导出表面的拓扑（流形性、全局连通性）与几何完整性。

Result: 引擎部署在两种设置下均降低图像质量；MeshSplatting 在 Standard Deployment 下退化最小；Dedicated Deployment 能保留大部分保真度，但运行时间约为 Standard Deployment 的 5–33 倍。mesh splatting 中显式连通性与共享索引仍受限，仅共享顶点索引既不能保证流形性也不能保证全局连通性。

Conclusion: 可光栅化只是"图形就绪"的一部分，引擎部署必须被显式评估；MeshSuite 为三角形/网格神经渲染的部署评估提供了系统化基准。

Abstract: Triangle- and mesh-based neural rendering seeks to bridge neural representations and graphics engines through triangle primitives compatible with standard rasterization. Yet existing methods were developed and evaluated under inconsistent settings, with few direct comparisons and little evaluation in graphics engines. We introduce MeshSuite, the first benchmark for systematic evaluation of engine deployment and Non-Engine Render in triangle- and mesh-based neural rendering. MeshSuite evaluates two deployment settings: Standard Deployment, using an opaque mesh pipeline with vertex colors and hardware Z-buffering; and Dedicated Deployment, using method-specific engine implementations to preserve appearance and compositing features. For mesh splatting, we also audit exported surfaces for topological and geometric integrity as downstream graphics assets. Our results show that engine deployment reduces image quality in both settings; among evaluated methods, MeshSplatting degrades least under Standard Deployment. Dedicated Deployment preserves most fidelity at roughly 5-33x the runtime of Standard Deployment across two deployment datasets. For mesh splatting, explicit connectivity and shared indexing remain limited: shared vertex indexing alone guarantees neither manifoldness nor global connectivity. These findings show that rasterizability is only part of graphics readiness and underscore the need to assess engine deployment. Project page: this https URL .
