### [1] [Background Bias Assessment and Mitigation in Plant Disease Detection Using EfficientNet-B0 with Image Processing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.00049)
*Majed Molhi,Nishi Gupta,Ali Algumaei*

Main category: cs.CV

TL;DR: 用三条件评估协议（原图/仅叶片/仅背景）定量揭示PlantVillage上植株病害检测模型存在背景偏置，提出背景偏置系数BBC=0.762，并验证EfficientNet-B0在仅背景图上准确率骤降至23.58%。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 基准数据集上99%+的病害检测精度可能掩盖模型利用虚假背景相关性（环境捷径）而非学习真实病理特征的问题，亟需可量化的偏置诊断手段。

Method: 两阶段训练框架：EfficientNet-B0迁移学习 + 优化过程中背景信息操控（背景替换/仅背景/仅叶片切分）；设计三条件评估协议（原图、仅叶片、仅背景）直接测量背景依赖，并据此定义BBC系数。

Result: PlantVillage（20,638图，15类）上原图98.97%，仅叶片96.62%，仅背景23.58%；BBC=0.762表明性能主要依赖叶片相关特征而非背景伪迹。

Conclusion: 即便先进模型也可能利用环境捷径，偏置评估应成为农业AI部署前的必要环节；代码与评估流程已公开。

Abstract: Plant disease detection using deep learning has achieved remarkable accuracies exceeding 99% on benchmark datasets. However, these metrics may mask a critical vulnerability: models may be taking advantage of false background correlations instead of learning real pathological features. This study presents a systematic investigation of background bias in plant disease detection through a two-stage training framework with quantitative validation. We propose a three-condition evaluation protocol testing models on original, leaf-only, and background-only images to directly measure background dependency. Our approach combines standard transfer learning using EfficientNet-B0 and manipulation of background information during optimization. Experimental results on the PlantVillage dataset (20,638 images, 15 disease classes) show that while our model achieves 98.97% accuracy on the original images and maintains 96.62% accuracy on leaf-only images, the performance drops to 23.58% on background-only images. The calculated background bias (BBC) coefficient of 0.762 indicates that a large part of the model's predictive performance depends on leaf-related features rather than background distortions. This research offers clear evidence that even with the most advanced models, environmental shortcuts can be leveraged, and this highlights the importance of bias evaluation. The approach that has been used has laid a foundation for the development of agricultural AI that is ready for deployment. The implementation, models, and evaluation process are all publicly available.

### [2] [CompliantBot: Learning Whole-Body Compliance across Task Interfaces for Humanoid Control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.01748)
*Haoyang Li,Keqi Zhu,Zhanxiang Cao,Yang Zhang,Yizhi Chen,Xuekang Yang,Yue Gao*

Main category: cs.RO

TL;DR: CompliantBot：无需合规数据或目标轨迹的人形机器人合规训练框架，通过状态相关交互力场耦合外力与运动、力幅奖励与任务奖励动态加权，实现"让步—恢复"平衡，支持动作跟踪与速度指令两类接口。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 人形控制器需在被外力推动时让步，又能恢复原任务；但跟踪目标会惩罚让步所需的偏差，长期放松跟踪又使恢复变难。现有方法依赖任务特定交互指令接口或人工构造的合规运动数据/参考轨迹，训练机制难以跨任务接口复用。

Method: 保留原任务输入，不构造合规数据或目标轨迹。构建状态相关交互力场，将施加负载映射为机器人所受的力，使局部关节与全身运动可改变受力；引入力幅奖励鼓励降低交互负载；加载时降低任务奖励权重、释放后恢复，协调让步与任务恢复。策略仅用任务输入与本体位姿历史，不显式测量外力。

Result: 在两类任务接口上，恒定拉力下让步距离更远、弹簧交互负载更低，撤力后可恢复任务跟踪；更强拉力下任务完成率最高；平均关节机械功率最低；真机演示展示手臂运动、迈步与身体转向等合规响应。

Conclusion: 提出一种通用、可跨任务接口复用的合规训练框架，无需外力传感与合规数据即可在仿真与真机上实现让步与任务恢复的平衡，并降低能耗。

Abstract: Humanoid controllers need the ability to yield to external forces while retaining the capacity to resume their original tasks. However, tracking objectives penalize the deviations needed for yielding, while relaxing tracking goals persistently can make task recovery harder. Existing approaches often rely on task-specific interaction command interfaces or constructed compliant motion data and reference trajectories, limiting the direct reuse of their compliance-training mechanisms across different task interfaces. We introduce CompliantBot, a compliance-training framework for motion tracking and velocity-command locomotion. It preserves the original task inputs and requires no preconstructed compliant data or target trajectories. A state-dependent interaction force field couples applied loads to the robot's motion, enabling local articulation and whole-body movement to change the forces experienced. A complementary force-magnitude reward encourages lower interaction loads, while task rewards are downweighted during loading and restored after release to coordinate yielding and task resumption. The trained policies use task inputs and proprioceptive history without explicit external-force measurements. Across both task interfaces, CompliantBot yields farther under constant pulls and experiences lower spring interaction loads than matched baselines, while resuming task tracking after force removal. CompliantBot achieves the highest task completion among the compared methods under stronger pulls. Power evaluations further show that CompliantBot has the lowest mean joint mechanical power among the compared methods. Real-robot demonstrations further illustrate compliant responses through arm motion, stepping, and body reorientation.
