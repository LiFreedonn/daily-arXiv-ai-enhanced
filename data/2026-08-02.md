### [1] [Shared Semantic Codebook Distillation for Unpaired Cross-Modal Medical Classification](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27357)
*Dillan Imans,Phuoc-Nguyen Bui,Duc-Tai Le,Hyunseung Choo*

Main category: cs.CV

TL;DR: 提出共享语义码本蒸馏(SSCD)，用于医学影像跨模态无配对知识蒸馏。通过共享离散码本将教师和学生表征映射为公共语义词汇上的分布，并全局及类别条件地对齐这些分布，无需配对样本或直接特征可比性。在OCT-to眼底和CT-to胸X光两个异质无配对场景中显著提升学生模型性能。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 跨模态知识蒸馏可以将诊断知识从强大但昂贵的教师模态转移到成本更低、更易部署的学生模态。但医学影像中两种模态通常不配对：来自不同患者群体，且占据几何不相容的特征空间，使得实例级蒸馏无效、直接特征匹配不可靠。

Method: SSCD通过共享离散码本对齐教师和学生表征。每个图像被表示为一个分布，覆盖在一个通用的、模态无关的词汇上；通过全局和类别条件方式跨模态对齐这些分布，无需配对样本或直接可比的原始特征。码本通过指数移动平均在线演化，并通过熵正则化和死码重启保持多样性。推理时仅保留学生编码器和分类器。

Result: 在OCT到眼底视网膜疾病分类中，学生模型从64.5提升到70.2 macro-F1；在CT到胸部X光肺炎分类中，从73.8提升到76.3 macro-F1，均优于所有评估的蒸馏基线。

Conclusion: SSCD有效解决了医学影像中无配对跨模态蒸馏问题，通过共享码本实现模态无关的知识对齐，且推理开销低（仅保留学生模型），代码和预训练模型已开源。

Abstract: Cross-modal knowledge distillation can transfer diagnostic knowledge from a strong but costly teacher modality to a cheaper and more deployable student modality. In medical image analysis, however, the two modalities are often unpaired: they are collected from different patient cohorts and occupy geometrically incompatible feature spaces. This makes instance-level distillation invalid and direct feature matching unreliable. To address these challenges, we propose Shared Semantic Codebook Distillation (SSCD), which compares teacher and student representations through a shared discrete codebook. Each image is represented as a distribution over a common, modality-agnostic vocabulary, and knowledge is transferred by aligning these distributions across modalities, both globally and class-conditionally, without requiring paired samples or directly comparable raw features. The codebook is evolved online by exponential moving average and kept diverse through entropy regularization and dead-code restart. At inference, all teacher-side and codebook modules are discarded, leaving only the student encoder and classifier. On two heterogeneous unpaired settings, OCT-to-fundus retinal disease classification and CT-to-chest-X-ray pneumonia classification, SSCD improves the student from 64.5 to 70.2 macro-F1 and from 73.8 to 76.3 macro-F1, respectively, outperforming all evaluated distillation baselines on both settings. Code and pretrained models are available at https://github.com/DillanImans/SSCD-unpaired-distillation

### [2] [PanDent: Toward Comprehensive Tooth-Level Structure-Language Consistency in Dental Radiology](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27378)
*Xiaohan Li,Xinyu Liu,Chang Liu,Sum Wing Au Yeung,Jun Liu,Yixuan Yuan,Hui Chen*

Main category: cs.CV

TL;DR: 本文提出PanDent，一个大规模、临床标注精细到牙齿级别的口腔全景X光片（OPG）基准数据集，包含9,524张高质量影像及专家验证的结构化标注，用于评估多模态大语言模型（MLLMs）在牙齿级诊断和临床一致性报告生成上的能力。实验表明当前MLLMs能生成流畅报告但临床一致性差，微调后显著提升。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有MLLM在牙科全景放射影像上的评估缺乏细粒度、临床可靠的基准，无法真实反映模型是否具备专家级解释能力。

Method: 构建包含9,524张OPG影像的数据集，由经验丰富的牙医生成精细牙齿级标注并由口腔颌面放射科医生验证，再通过临床逻辑生成结构与文本一致的报告。在多种MLLM（专有SOTA、通用开源、医学专用）上进行评测。

Result: 当前MLLMs能生成流畅报告但临床一致描述能力差，在细粒度定位和牙齿级诊断上错误明显；在PanDent上微调可显著提升结构-语言一致性、视觉定位精度和诊断正确性。

Conclusion: PanDent为MLLMs的牙齿级临床推理提供了严谨基准，是临床牙科AI的有价值资源。

Abstract: Accurate evaluation of multimodal large language models (MLLMs) in dental panoramic radiography (orthopantomogram, OPG) is limited by the lack of fine-grained, clinically reliable benchmarks that reflect expert interpretation. This work introduces PanDent, a large-scale, clinically grounded OPG benchmark built upon fine-grained, expert-validated tooth-level annotations. The dataset comprises 9,524 high-quality OPGs, each associated with comprehensive structured annotations produced by experienced dentists and further validated by an oral and maxillofacial radiologist, providing clinically reliable supervision for tooth-level diagnosis and reasoning. Clinically consistent radiology reports are constructed from expert-validated findings using clinician-defined reporting logic, establishing explicit correspondence between structured clinical evidence and free-text descriptions. This design enables evaluation of whether MLLMs generate reports that are not only linguistically coherent but also clinically consistent with expert-validated tooth-level findings. Experiments are conducted on diverse MLLMs, including state-of-the-art (SOTA) proprietary models, general-domain open-source models, and medical-specific models. Results show that current MLLMs can generate fluent reports, yet fail to produce clinically consistent descriptions, exhibiting substantial errors in fine-grained localization and tooth-level diagnosis. Fine-tuning on PanDent significantly improves structure-language consistency, substantially enhancing visual localization accuracy and diagnostic correctness, and bringing model outputs closer to expert dental interpretation. These results establish PanDent as a rigorous benchmark for evaluating tooth-level clinical reasoning in MLLMs and a valuable resource for clinically grounded dental AI.

### [3] [IGME: Efficient Chained Method Ensemble for Transferable Semantic Segmentation Attacks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27465)
*Mengqi He,Jing Zhang*

Main category: cs.CV

TL;DR: 本文提出IGME，一种仅需单个源模型的高效可迁移对抗攻击方法，用于语义分割模型。通过将可迁移攻击组合公式化为可微攻击组件的链式计算以共享源模型梯度，并用积分梯度风格的路经平均方向作为稳定化启发式。在Pascal VOC和Cityscapes上，IGME在迁移性上可与单源基线竞争，在运行时间上优于模型集成攻击。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 语义分割模型易受可迁移对抗扰动攻击，但评估密集预测模型的迁移攻击计算开销大。现有集成攻击依赖多个代理模型，进一步增加计算成本。需要一个高效的单源替代方案来实现可迁移攻击。

Method: 将可迁移攻击组合公式化为可微攻击组件上的链式计算，使昂贵的源模型梯度计算可以共享；采用积分梯度风格的路经平均方向作为经验性稳定化启发式来降低链式组合带来的更新不稳定性。

Result: 在Pascal VOC和Cityscapes上，IGME在迁移性与单源基线相当，运行时间优于模型集成攻击，且仅需访问一个源模型。

Conclusion: IGME实现了迁移性与效率的良好平衡，证明了单源模型在可迁移语义分割攻击上的可行性。

Abstract: Semantic segmentation models are vulnerable to transferable adversarial perturbations, yet evaluating transfer attacks on dense prediction models can be computationally expensive. Existing ensemble attacks often rely on multiple surrogate models, increasing the computation cost, even harder for segmentation. This paper studies an efficient single-source alternative for transferable attacks on semantic segmentation. We formulate transferable attack composition as a chained computation over differentiable attack components, allowing the expensive source-model gradient computation to be shared. To reduce the update instability introduced by chained composition, we further use an integrated-gradient-style path-averaged direction as an empirical stabilization heuristic. Experiments on Pascal VOC and Cityscapes evaluate the resulting transferability efficiency trade-off across CNN- and transformer-based segmentation models. IGME achieves competitive transferability compared with single-source baselines and favorable runtime compared with model-ensemble attacks, while requiring access to only one source model.

### [4] [Objective-Aligned Direct Answer SFT for Robust Multi-Frame Medical VQA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27566)
*Site Li,Jianyi Hao,Xiaofeng Liu*

Main category: cs.CV

TL;DR: 在MedFrameQA多帧医学视觉问答基准上，系统对比了多种复杂适应方法（控制器式推理、定位感知重排、静态硬负例混合、分阶段持续训练等），发现直接解码器式答案监督微调（SFT）是最强且最鲁棒的适应族，显著优于冻结基线，且在重复种子和匹配控制下保持稳定，事后校准可修复置信度估计而不损失准确率。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 多帧医学VQA领域倾向于奖励日益复杂的架构适应（控制器、重排、硬负例混合、分阶段持续训练等），但作者质疑这些复杂机制是否真的必要，希望检验一个更简单的假设：与基准最终答案目标紧密对齐的方法在受控评估下应是最强的鲁棒适应族。

Method: 在MedFrameQA上系统对比五类方法：基于控制器的方法、脚手架进化、静态混合监督、持续训练变体、以及直接仅答案监督微调（SFT）。评估受控条件包括固定划分、匹配预算、重复种子和校准。使用MedGemma-1.5-4B为主干，Qwen2.5-VL-3B为次要骨干验证迁移性，并采用事后校准修复置信度。

Result: 直接解码器仅答案SFT是最强鲁棒适应族，在留出集报告准确率上大幅超越冻结基线；跨重复种子和匹配控制下保持显著稳定，证明是家族级鲁棒性而非孤立超参数点；事后校准有效修复置信度估计且不牺牲准确率；方法可迁移至Qwen2.5-VL-3B等次要骨干。

Conclusion: 核心结论不是复杂辅助机制获胜，而是目标对齐的直接答案SFT是MedFrameQA上最强鲁棒适应族。作者希望以此极简强基线，引导社区关注根本性鲁棒优化而非架构复杂度。

Abstract: Multi-frame medical VQA appears to reward increasingly complex adaptation: controller-style inference, localization-aware reranking, static hard-negative mixing, and staged continuation all appear plausible from first principles. We test a simpler competing hypothesis on MedFrameQA: methods that remain tightly aligned with the benchmark's final answer objective should be the strongest \emph{robust} adaptation family once evaluation is controlled across fixed splits, matched budgets, repeated seeds, and calibration. We compare controller-based methods, scaffold evolution, static mixed supervision, continuation-heavy variants, and direct answer-only supervised fine-tuning (SFT). The strongest robust family is direct decoder-only answer SFT on MedGemma-1.5-4B. Empirically, this family yields substantial improvements in held-out report accuracy over frozen baselines while remaining remarkably stable across repeated seeds and matched controls, ensuring our claims reflect true family-level robustness rather than an isolated hyperparameter peak. Furthermore, post-hoc calibration effectively repairs confidence estimation without compromising accuracy, and the core approach transfers consistently to secondary backbones like Qwen2.5-VL-3B. The main result is therefore not that a complex auxiliary mechanism wins, but that objective-aligned direct answer SFT is the strongest robust adaptation family we found for MedFrameQA. By establishing this strong, minimalist baseline, we hope to redirect community focus toward fundamentally robust optimization rather than architectural complexity.

### [5] [ZMIS-SAM: Segment Anything Model Enhanced with Wavelet Transform for Zooplankton Microscopy Image Instance Segmentation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27585)
*Dekun Yuan,Zhongwei Li,Zheng Qiao,Jie Zhang*

Main category: cs.CV

TL;DR: 本文提出ZMIS-SAM，一种基于SAM和 wavelet transform 的浮游动物显微图像实例分割模型，通过轻量适配器增强SAM的领域建模能力，并设计了NFAM和WM2FE模块分别解决细长附肢的连续分割和边界完整性恢复问题，在浮游动物数据集上达到SOTA性能并具备跨域泛化能力。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: SAM作为通用分割模型缺乏浮游动物领域知识，在显微图像实例分割中表现有限，存在分类不准、细长附肢分割不连续、边界分割不完整等问题，需构建领域特化的分割模型。

Method: 提出ZMIS-SAM框架，包含三大核心创新：(1) ZM-ViT通过两个轻量适配器增强SAM对浮游动物形态和图像强度分布的建模能力；(2) 邻域特征聚合模块(NFAM)整合通用与领域特征以改善半透明细长附肢的连续分割；(3) 基于小波变换的多尺度多方向特征增强模块(WM2FE)恢复高频细节以细化边界分割。

Abstract: As primary consumers in the marine food chain, zooplankton play a crucial role in maintaining marine ecological balance. However, the Segment Anything Model (SAM) exhibits limited performance in microscopic image instance segmentation due to its lack of zooplankton-specific domain knowledge. To address these challenges, we propose a novel instance segmentation model based on SAM and wavelet transform (ZMIS-SAM), effectively tackling issues such as inaccurate classification, discontinuous segmentation of slender appendages, and incomplete boundary segmentation. Our framework incorporates three core innovations: ZM-ViT enhances SAM's capability to model zooplankton morphology and image intensity distributions through two lightweight adapters, the Neighboring Feature Aggregation Module (NFAM) improves continuous segmentation of semi-transparent slender appendages by integrating general-purpose and domain-specific features, and the Wavelet-based Multi-scale Multi-directional Feature Enhancement (WM2FE) module effectively recovers high-frequency details to refine boundary segmentation completeness. Extensive experiments demonstrate that ZMIS-SAM achieves state-of-the-art instance segmentation performance on the zooplankton dataset and exhibits strong generalization capability across multiple public cross-domain datasets.

### [6] [RaDiVe: Robust 4D Radar Odometry with Distance-Bounded NDT and Velocity-Discrepancy Point Uncertainty](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28045)
*Sangwoo Jung,Dongjae Lee,Chiyun Noh,Ayoung Kim*

Main category: cs.RO

TL;DR: RaDiVe是一个4D雷达里程计框架，通过距离受限NDT减少计算、基于速度差异的逐点不确定性权重处理测量模糊性，以及SDF隐式神经映射提取几何一致的表面点用于子图构建，在多个公开数据集上相比现有基线在平移ATE上降低44.4%、旋转ATE降低21.3%，且保持实时性能。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 4D雷达在恶劣天气下具备鲁棒感知能力，但雷达点云固有的稀疏性、噪声和有限的位置精度使得基于配准的里程计面临显著挑战，现有方法在精度和鲁棒性上不足。

Method: 1) 距离受限NDT（distance-bounded NDT）：限制对应搜索到近距离体素对，提升优化稳定性和计算效率；2) 速度差异点不确定性模型：根据测量多普勒径向速度与从估计自身速度预测的径向速度之间的差异为每个点加权；3) 基于SDF隐式神经映射的表面点提取，构建几何一致且噪声过滤的局部子图。

Result: 在多个公开数据集上，RaDiVe相比现有4D雷达里程计基线，平均平移ATE降低44.4%，旋转ATE降低21.3%，同时保持实时性能。

Conclusion: RaDiVe通过组合创新的配准优化、不确定性感知加权和子图构建技术，显著提升了4D雷达里程计的精度和鲁棒性，且代码将开源。

Abstract: Recent advances in 4D radar enable robust perception in adverse weather; however, the inherent sparsity, noise, and limited positional precision of radar point clouds pose significant challenges for registration-based odometry. In this letter, we propose RaDiVe, a 4D radar odometry framework designed to improve the accuracy and robustness of radar point-cloud registration. We introduce a distance-bounded Normal Distributions Transform (NDT), which improves optimization stability and computational efficiency by restricting the correspondence search to near-distance voxel pairs. To mitigate measurement ambiguity, we propose a velocity-discrepancy point uncertainty model that weights each input 4D radar point according to the discrepancy between its measured Doppler radial velocity and the radial velocity predicted from the estimated ego-velocity. Furthermore, we incorporate Signed Distance Function (SDF)-based surface point extraction via implicit neural mapping to construct a geometrically consistent and noise-filtered local submap. Evaluations across multiple public datasets demonstrate that RaDiVe outperforms existing 4D radar odometry baselines by 44.4% in translational Absolute Trajectory Error (ATE) and 21.3% in rotational ATE on average, while maintaining real-time performance. The source code will be made publicly available to the robotics community: https://github.com/to-be-open-sourced.

</details>
