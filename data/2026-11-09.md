### [1] [Visual Forensic Detection of Fabricated Bangla News Photocards using Deep Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05403)
*Kundu, Anindya,Saha, Kabya Mithun,Islam, Md. Muhaiminul,Ahmed, Fayek,Bulbul, Aminul Kader,Ibrahim, Muhammad*

Main category: cs.CV

TL;DR: 该论文将孟加拉语"照片卡"（photocard）假新闻检测重构为视觉图像伪造检测任务，构建了969张真实+1077张伪造的Bangla photocard数据集（经16种光度/形态变换扩充至32,736样本），比较了全连接ANN、紧凑4块CNN（489,954参数）与ImageNet预训练YOLOv8n-cls，并采用无泄漏分层分组五折交叉验证。结果：ANN约52%（损失卡在ln2），紧凑CNN从头训练87.45%±0.53%、分类头热启动88.75%±1.55%，YOLOv8n-cls微调达97.36%±0.42%。

Motivation: 孟加拉国大量地方化虚假信息以"照片卡"形式传播——单图新闻摘要模仿知名新闻机构的品牌视觉，误导信息存在于像素中而非机器可读文本，因此纯文本事实核查工具无法识别。作者据此将photocard假新闻检测重新表述为视觉图像伪造检测问题，以利用空间伪造线索。

Method: 数据来自公开Facebook页面的抓取：真实性卡片取自17家全国电视广播机构的认证页面，伪造卡片取自伪广播机构页面、匿名账号和公开群组。每张图进行16种光度与形态学变换扩充语料。模型包括：(1) 全连接ANN作为基线；(2) 紧凑型全批归一化四块CNN（489,954参数，较ANN小约1,644倍），采用保留同一卡片所有增强版本于同一折的无泄漏分层分组五折交叉验证；(3) ImageNet预训练的YOLOv8n-cls（1.44M参数）在同折微调作为性能上界参照。报告逐类precision/recall/F1与汇总混淆矩阵，并对未归一化分类头导致的单类坍缩进行诊断与修复。

Result: ANN准确率仅约52%，损失停滞在ln(2)≈0.693，表明展平图像破坏了伪造检测所需的空间线索。紧凑CNN从头训练达87.45%±0.53%，分类头由ANN热启动后达88.75%±1.55%；YOLOv8n-cls达97.36%±0.42%，显示紧凑模型之上仍有显著提升空间。偏差-方差诊断表明紧凑CNN处于欠拟合状态。

Conclusion: 将photocard假新闻检测转化为视觉伪造检测是可行且有效的；空间结构对判别至关重要，纯全连接结构完全失效。紧凑CNN在极低参数量下已具备较强性能，但预训练骨干揭示了约10个百分点的性能上限差距，说明受限于容量与数据规模。作者同时提供了错误分析与偏差-方差诊断，明确指出紧凑模型欠拟合，为后续引入预训练或更强正则/容量配置提供依据。

Abstract: In Bangladesh, much localized misinformation spreads as photocards: single-image news summaries that imitate the branding of well-known news organizations. Because the misleading message lives in the pixels rather than in machine-readable text, text-based fact-checking tools cannot see it. We recast photocard fake-news detection as visual image forgery detection. We build a dataset of 969 authentic and 1,077 fabricated Bangla photocards scraped from public Facebook pages: authentic cards from the verified pages of seventeen national television broadcasters, fabricated ones from pseudo-broadcaster pages, anonymous profiles, and public groups. Sixteen photometric and morphological transformations per image grow the corpus to 32,736 samples. A fully connected ANN reaches only about 52% accuracy, its loss stuck at ln(2) ~ 0.693, showing that flattening the image destroys the spatial cues forgery detection needs. A compact, fully batch-normalized four-block CNN with 489,954 parameters, about 1,644x smaller than the ANN, is evaluated with leakage-free stratified group five-fold cross-validation, which keeps every augmented version of a card in one fold. Under this protocol the CNN reaches 87.45% +/- 0.53% pooled accuracy from scratch and 88.75% +/- 1.55% when its classifier head is warm-started from the ANN. An ImageNet-pretrained YOLOv8n-cls backbone (1.44M parameters) fine-tuned on the same folds reaches 97.36% +/- 0.42%, marking the headroom above the compact model. We report per-class precision, recall, F1, and pooled confusion matrices, diagnose and fix a one-class collapse caused by an unnormalized classifier head, and give a manual error analysis and bias-variance diagnosis showing that the compact CNNs under-fit.
